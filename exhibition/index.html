The Action of the Click

Curated by Joshua Batista

This exhibition, The Action of the Click, explores how the cursor serves as the primary tool for narrative and social commentary in net art. By bringing together We Become What We Behold, Pointer Pointer, and My Boyfriend Came Back From the War, the show demonstrates how the user's physical interaction with the interface is necessary for the artwork to exist. These works are connected by their reliance on the "point-and-click" mechanism to uncover layers of a story or to trigger a reaction from the network. Whether the user is capturing sensationalized news or navigating a fragmented conversation through HTML frames, the aesthetic strategy focuses on the tension between the viewer and the screen. This collection positions the click as a deliberate act of participation rather than just a way to navigate a page.

We Become What We Behold (2016)
Artist: Nicky Case

An interactive project where the player clicks to take photos of characters, showing how the media's choice of what to capture creates a cycle of social tension and viral behavior.

Pointer Pointer (2010)
Artist: Studio Moniker

A project that tracks the user's cursor and finds an image of a person pointing exactly at its location, highlighting the relationship between the user and the vast archive of the web.
